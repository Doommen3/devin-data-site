Operations & Automation

Paper inventory → a system that runs itself

A multi-location specialty coffee roaster · ongoing
The problem

Staff counted inventory on paper and re-keyed it by hand every week.

What I built

An offline-first inventory app employees install on their phones. Every count writes to both a permanent ledger and the live ordering grid, with automatic weekly rollover, one-click setup for new locations, and a manager command-center dashboard. Plus email automation across the company's accounts and a searchable employee manual built from 16 source documents.

The result

Paper counts replaced with a phone tap · weekly rollover automated · live across locations.

Operations Platform

10 disconnected systems → one platform

A multi-location medical practice
The problem

The practice ran on 10 separate systems. Prepping a single patient-day took two full days of manual work.

What I built

One platform for scheduling, check-in, KPI tracking, and daily reports. ETL pipelines pull live data straight from their practice-management software into a 30+ metric reporting framework.

The result

2 days of manual prep → 30 minutes · ~30+ hours/week of manual data collection eliminated.
